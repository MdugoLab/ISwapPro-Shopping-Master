ISwapPro Shopping Master V26
HTTPS Camera Deployment & Android Verification Milestone

Built from the verified V25 project. This milestone preserves the V25 shopping features and prepares the project for secure-host camera testing.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- General Shopping, Business Items, and Tools & Project Items remain separate purchasing lists.
- Category selection remains available in Add Item, Inventory, and Insights.
- Barcode entry and the existing camera scanner are preserved for shopping items and inventory.
- Camera diagnostics explain secure-context, camera API, permission, and BarcodeDetector limitations.
- Embedded CSS/JavaScript remain synchronised with css/styles.css and js/app.js.
- Existing localStorage key is retained: iswappro_shopping_cart_v1.

CAMERA REQUIREMENTS
Live camera scanning requires: (1) HTTPS or localhost, (2) Chrome camera permission, (3) navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia support, and (4) BarcodeDetector support for a product barcode format. A local Android file opened through content:// may not qualify as a secure context. A permission prompt alone does not guarantee barcode decoding support.

IMPORTANT TEST STATUS
Static JavaScript syntax, embedded-source synchronisation, and ZIP integrity are checked. Live camera operation has NOT yet been verified on the user's Android device; that must be tested after deployment over HTTPS.
